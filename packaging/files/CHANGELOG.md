# Changelog

All notable changes to Pen & Ink Tracker are documented here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-06

Initial public release.

- Track fountain pens, inks, and papers in three local collections, with
  filters, grid/list views, search, and photo galleries.
- Swab wall: hue-sorted color wall built from ink swab photos.
- Photo tagging: Photo / Swab / Writing sample, auto-picked by the UI.
- "Currently inked" tracking with a dashboard and a flush-reminder nudge.
- Import wizard for pasting rows from Excel/Google Sheets or a CSV.
- CSV export per collection and ready-to-post sale listing text.
- Optional AI auto-fill and photo finder via Google Gemini's free tier.
- Installable as a PWA, including a phone camera hand-off via QR code.
- Ships with a ~4,900-entry ink / ~380-entry pen / ~835-entry paper seed
  catalog for autocomplete (see `CREDITS.md` for sources and licensing).
